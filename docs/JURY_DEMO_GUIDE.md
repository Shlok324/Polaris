# POLARIS: 5-Minute SIH Jury Demonstration Script
## Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement 26063

Follow this exact flow to deliver a 5-minute presentation that hits all evaluation criteria:

---

### Step 1: Open the Landing Page (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Showcase**: The warm polar expedition journal aesthetic (earth tones, serif headings, topographic lines).
- **Pitch**: *"Judges, polar research is distributed across decades of separate expedition reports, obscure NetCDF datasets, and institutional vaults. POLARIS is India's centralized digital ecosystem that unifies our Antarctic, Arctic, Southern Ocean, and Himalayan knowledge."*
- **Action**: Interact with the **Hero Globe**, toggling between **Antarctica (69°S)**, **Arctic (79°N)**, and **Third Pole**, showing animated voyage routes from Goa to Larsemann Hills.
- **Action**: Highlight the National Polar Statistics: **42+ Expeditions**, **2 Antarctic Stations**, **1,000+ Publications**, and **500+ Datasets**.
- **Action**: Scroll through **The Challenge (5 numbered cards)** and the **Central Workflow diagram** (*Discover → Organize → Understand → Explore → Learn → Disseminate*).

---

### Step 2: The Knowledge Repository & Semantic AI Search (0:45 - 1:45)
- **Action**: Click **Repository** in the top navigation.
- **Pitch**: *"Researchers and students can discover peer-reviewed papers and calibrated raw datasets."*
- **Action**: In the search bar, click the preset semantic chip:
  > **"What research was conducted at Bharati Station related to climate?"**
- **Showcase**: Notice how POLARIS AI immediately synthesizes a structured summary with inline citations `[1]`, `[2]`, key findings (-4.2 cm/decade fast-ice thinning), and filters to matching publications.
- **Action**: On the first card (*Decadal Trends in Antarctic Fast-Ice Thickness*), click **"Explain Simply"**.
- **Showcase**: Toggle between:
  - **School Student**: Plain English, everyday wool scarf analogy, glossary.
  - **College Student**: Thermodynamic equation concepts and conductive damping.
  - **Research Summary**: CMIP6 model integration implications.

---

### Step 3: Interactive Polar Map & Station Telemetry (1:45 - 2:30)
- **Action**: Click **Polar Map** in the navbar.
- **Showcase**: View the custom polar stereographic projection with expedition route lines from Goa and Cape Town.
- **Action**: Click the **Bharati** station marker.
- **Showcase**: The detail drawer reveals live simulated sensor telemetry (temperature: -18.4°C, wind speed, barometric pressure), geographic coordinates (69°24'S, 76°11'E), and key facilities.
- **Action**: Click **Explore Station** to view the full station timeline (2006 site survey → 2012 commissioning → 2024 atmospheric LIDAR).

---

### Step 4: Virtual Polar Experience (2:30 - 3:15)
- **Action**: Navigate to **Explore** page and scroll to **Virtual Station Explorer**.
- **Pitch**: *"To bridge the engagement gap for schools and the public, we engineered a lightweight interactive station visualizer."*
- **Action**: Click **Rotate 45°**, then click on interactive hotspots:
  - 🔬 **Laboratory Complex**: Cleanroom filtration specs.
  - 🏠 **Living Quarters**: Insulated container architecture.
  - 🛰️ **Ka-Band Satellite Dome**: 100 Mbps uplink to NCPOR Goa.
  - ⚡ **Zero-Effluent Treatment Hub**: Zero waste discharge compliance under the Antarctic Treaty Madrid Protocol.

---

### Step 5: Education Academy & Working Quiz (3:15 - 4:00)
- **Action**: Click **Learn** in the navbar.
- **Action**: Open the featured lesson: **"Life at an Antarctic Research Station"**.
- **Showcase**: Walk through the sections (Architecture, Daily Life, Polar Night isolation).
- **Action**: Scroll to the interactive **Module Mastery Assessment**:
  - Answer Question 1: *"Why is Bharati on stilts?"* → Select *"To allow howling blizzard snowdrifts to blow beneath without burying the station"*.
  - Complete all questions and click **Submit Answers**.
- **Showcase**: The confetti animation triggers, showing a 100% score, instant scientific explanations, and `+100 XP` awarded to the user's gamification profile!

---

### Step 6: Media Dissemination Studio (4:00 - 4:35)
- **Action**: Click **Media** in the navbar.
- **Pitch**: *"The biggest gap in government research is converting scientific findings into accessible outreach. In POLARIS Media Studio, one click does this automatically."*
- **Action**: Select a paper and toggle:
  - **Instagram Carousel**: Multi-slide visual story with caption and hashtags.
  - **60s Video Script**: Complete with timecodes, drone shot descriptions, and voiceover narration.
  - **LinkedIn Professional Article**: Formal policy-relevant impact summary.
- **Action**: Click **Copy Content** to show outreach readiness.

---

### Step 7: Analytics & Admin CMS (4:35 - 5:00)
- **Action**: Click **Analytics** to show the interactive Recharts dashboard (Monthly Access Trends, Regional Distribution Donut, Content Breakdown Bar Chart, Top Search Queries).
- **Action**: Click the User Profile in top right, switch role to **Admin**, and open **Admin CMS**.
- **Showcase**: Show the full content management table with workflow statuses (Draft, In Review, Published) and the modal to add new publications and datasets.
- **Concluding Statement**: *"POLARIS fulfills SIH Problem 26063 by connecting researchers, students, educators, media, and institutions into one cohesive, discoverable, and shareable ecosystem for India's polar science."*
